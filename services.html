SERVICE 03
3D Scanning & Reverse Engineering

Assistance in identifying suitable scanning solutions and coordinating workflows to convert physical components or scan data into usable CAD deliverables.

Our specialised support:
  • 3D Scanning Coordination: Review component size, geometry, material, surface finish and accuracy requirements.
  • Scan-to-CAD: Coordinate conversion of scan data or mesh files into editable CAD geometry.
  • Reverse Engineering: Support reconstruction from physical parts, dimensions, drawings or scan data.
  • CAD Deliverables: STL, OBJ, STEP, IGES, native CAD files and agreed inspection reports.
Typical applications:
  • Legacy component digitisation.
  • Replacement-part development.
  • Tooling and fixture reconstruction.
  • Product design modification.
  • Geometry comparison and inspection support.

Deliverables and accuracy expectations are confirmed based on component complexity, data quality and agreed project scope.
