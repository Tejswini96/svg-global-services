About SVG Global Services

Connecting Engineering Knowledge With Business Needs

SVG Global Services is an engineering and industrial services business focused on staffing, mechanical design, reverse engineering and additive manufacturing consultancy.

We aim to support organisations that require skilled technical resources, external engineering capabilities or assistance identifying appropriate industrial solutions.

Our business approach combines mechanical engineering knowledge with commercial understanding to help customers address practical challenges in product development, manufacturing and workforce planning.
